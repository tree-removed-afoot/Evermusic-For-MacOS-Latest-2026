### ❔ What is Evermusic for Mac?

🎵 Evermusic is a cloud and local music player for Mac that can organize and play music from cloud storage, NAS devices, computers, media servers, USB sources, and local files.

### ❔ Can I listen to music offline?

📥 Yes. Supported tracks and collections can be downloaded for offline playback.

### ❔ Does Evermusic work with NAS devices?

🗄️ Yes. Evermusic supports compatible NAS connections through protocols such as SMB, WebDAV, FTP, SFTP, NFS, and DLNA.

### ❔ Can I connect Google Drive or Dropbox?

☁️ Yes. Evermusic supports Google Drive, Dropbox, OneDrive, iCloud Drive, MEGA, Box, pCloud, and other supported cloud services.

🔄 Yes. Gapless playback is available for supported audio.

📋 Yes. Evermusic includes a playlist manager for creating, organizing, reordering, and exporting supported playlists.
